# Pivot Sports Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** April 28, 2026

Pivot Sports ("we", "our", or "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Pivot Sports mobile and web applications.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

### Account Information

- Email address
- Display name

### User-Generated Content

- Teams you create or join
- Events and RSVPs
- Messages and discussions within teams

### Device Information

- Device tokens (for push notifications)
- Basic app usage data

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Create and manage your account
- Allow you to join and manage teams
- Enable communication within teams
- Send notifications (such as event reminders and updates)
- Improve app performance and user experience

---

## 3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is securely stored using third-party infrastructure (Supabase).

We take reasonable measures to protect your information; however, no system is completely secure.

---

## 4. Sharing of Information

We do **not sell your personal data**.

We only share information:

- With other users within your teams (e.g., your name and messages)
- With service providers required to operate the app (e.g., hosting and authentication services)

---

## 5. Push Notifications

Pivot Sports may send notifications such as:

- Event reminders
- Team updates
- Discussion activity

You can manage your notification preferences within the app.

---

## 6. Data Retention

We retain your data as long as your account is active.

If you leave a team or delete your account, some information may remain where necessary (for example, messages for team continuity).

---

## 7. Your Rights

You may:

- Update your profile information
- Leave teams
- Request account deletion by contacting us

---

## 8. Children's Privacy

Pivot Sports is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will take steps to delete it.

---

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted with a revised effective date.
